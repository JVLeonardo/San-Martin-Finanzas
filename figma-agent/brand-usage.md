# Official brand asset usage

Use only the assets inside `references/`. They are copied official derivatives for Figma use, not files to redraw, recolor, crop, trace, or replace. Content and task hierarchy always come first; identity comes second; regional graphics come third.

## Logos

| Asset | Use | Do not use |
|---|---|---|
| `references/logos/smf-logo-horizontal-1600.png` | Light desktop header and institutional light surfaces. | Mobile headers, dark surfaces, or as recreated text. |
| `references/logos/smf-logo-horizontal-negative.png` | Dark desktop headers or Forest 900 institutional surfaces. | Light surfaces. |
| `references/logos/smf-logo-compact-145.png` | Mobile header, 125–145 px wide reference. | Dense form interiors or as a favicon. |
| `references/logos/smf-logo-compact-negative.png` | Dark mobile header or dark compact brand panel. | Light surfaces. |

Maintain generous clear space. Do not stretch, recolor, apply effects, place on visually noisy imagery, or combine multiple logo variants in one view.

## Symbols

| Asset | Use | Do not use |
|---|---|---|
| `references/symbols/smf-isotype-256.png` | Only as a fallback mobile identity mark when the compact logo cannot remain legible. | Decorative repetition, navigation icons, or a substitute wordmark. |
| `references/symbols/smf-symbol-256.png` | Small, real UI identity use such as a compact profile/product marker where no functional icon conflict exists. | General decoration, financial status icons, app/PWA metadata, or favicon replacement. |

## Regional graphics

| Asset | Expected Figma use | Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| `references/graphics/smf-route-primary-forest900-1600.png` | Warm White/light editorial section or identity block. | One primary resource per composition; never a map, arrow, chart, or repeated background. |
| `references/graphics/smf-route-primary-white-1600.png` | Forest 900 hero, CTA, or dark institutional surface. | Never combine as a protagonist with horizon or photography. |
| `references/graphics/smf-topography-light-forest700.png` | Level 1 subtle texture on a light section. | Do not make it content, use in fields, or extend/tile it. |
| `references/graphics/smf-topography-dark-white.png` | Level 1 subtle texture on a Forest 900/dark section. | Do not use with another protagonist graphic. |
| `references/graphics/smf-horizon-light-desktop-1600.png` | Level 2 desktop hero, institutional section, or footer background. | Retain its palette; no dark recolor. |
| `references/graphics/smf-horizon-mobile.png` | Level 2 responsive mobile composition when the desktop aspect ratio does not fit. | Do not place behind dense form controls or add competing graphics. |

No approved dark horizon exists. Use a dark surface with the White route or dark Topography when appropriate; do not invent a horizon recolor. Route extension and seamless Topography are also unavailable.

## Photography

No final approved or licensed photograph is supplied. `references/brand-guidelines/7. Dirección Fotográfica de Marca.png` is direction only. If the future Figma exercise uses temporary imagery, label it as prototype/reference-only and do not present it as a real customer, official content, or approved brand photography.

Prioritize real people, daily work/community contexts, natural light, calm trust, and useful negative space. Reject stock banking poses, money, cards, tourism, wildlife, poverty dramatization, luxury, or AI-generated final imagery.

## Normative plates

The six included guideline plates are consultation material. They prevent reinterpretation of logo variants, color/typography, correct usage, regional-graphic hierarchy, photography direction, and digital application. They are not screen content and must not be used as UI backgrounds.
