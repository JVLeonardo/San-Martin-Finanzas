# Reference Folder Guidance

This folder is intentionally empty until approved visual references are selected. References are **inspiration, not templates to copy**. Figma Agent must extract principles—such as composition, restraint, typography, or image direction—without reproducing another brand, copy, layout, or product claim.

Add future approved references to these categories:

```text
references/
├── 01-brand-mood/
├── 02-photography/
├── 03-layout/
├── 04-financial-ui/
├── 05-regional-identity/
└── 06-do-not-copy/
```

## `01-brand-mood/`

Look for restrained fintech, editorial clarity, generous whitespace, deep green use, and contemporary financial institutions. Extract tone, contrast, hierarchy, and restraint—not logos or branded shapes.

## `02-photography/`

Look for documentary photography of workers, merchants, small businesses, natural mobile-phone use, natural light, and credible expressions. Avoid staged corporate stock, money imagery, tourism, luxury, dramatized poverty, and stereotypes.

## `03-layout/`

Look for asymmetric heroes, editorial layouts, typographic hierarchy, alternating surfaces, and compositions that avoid unnecessary card grids. Extract responsive hierarchy and whitespace decisions.

## `04-financial-ui/`

Look for clear disclosure structure, calculators, repayment information, and financial forms. Do not copy financial content, formulas, rates, terms, or claims. The Sprint 1 simulator remains a non-calculating structural placeholder.

## `05-regional-identity/`

Look for abstract cartography, topographic curves, river lines, restrained vegetation abstraction, and regional color cues. Interpret identity subtly; do not create tourist imagery or literal jungle decoration.

## `06-do-not-copy/`

Collect examples of purple/blue fintech gradients, glassmorphism, crypto styling, excessive card systems, banknote imagery, fake dashboards, and generic SaaS templates. Use them only as anti-pattern evidence.

When a reference conflicts with the approved product direction, Sprint 1 scope, accessibility, or this package, this package wins.
