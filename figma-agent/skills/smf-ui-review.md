\# San Martín Finanzas — UI Review



Review the selected design as a senior Product/UI Designer.



Do not redesign automatically unless explicitly asked.



Evaluate:



\## 1. Brand

\- Does it clearly feel like San Martín Finanzas?

\- Are approved colors and typography used correctly?

\- Is Earth Gold restrained?

\- Are logo and graphic assets used correctly?



\## 2. Visual hierarchy

\- Is the primary action obvious?

\- Is the reading order clear?

\- Is typography hierarchy strong?

\- Is whitespace intentional?



\## 3. Originality

\- Does it avoid generic SaaS/fintech template aesthetics?

\- Is there excessive card stacking?

\- Does the composition have personality?



\## 4. UX

\- Are actions understandable?

\- Are forms clear?

\- Are states understandable?

\- Is important information easy to scan?



\## 5. Consistency

\- Are spacing, radius, typography and components consistent?

\- Are reusable components being used instead of manual duplicates?



\## 6. Content

\- Is all visible UI text in Spanish (Peru)?

\- Are there unsupported financial, legal, regulatory or security claims?

\- Is placeholder/reference information clearly identified?



\## 7. Accessibility

\- Contrast

\- focus

\- touch targets

\- readable text

\- clear errors

\- logical order



Return the review using only:



STATUS:

PASS / REFINE / REBUILD



BLOCKERS:

Only critical issues.



IMPORTANT IMPROVEMENTS:

Prioritized issues.



OPTIONAL POLISH:

Non-blocking refinements.



Do not praise generically. Be specific and actionable.
