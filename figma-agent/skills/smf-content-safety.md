\# San Martín Finanzas — Content Safety



Use this skill whenever creating or editing financial, product, legal, simulator, trust, security or application-related content.



Do not invent definitive business information.



Never invent or present as approved:

\- interest rates

\- TCEA

\- min/max loan amounts

\- installment values

\- fees

\- commissions

\- insurance

\- penalties

\- moratorium rules

\- approval criteria

\- scoring rules

\- approval times

\- disbursement times

\- regulatory status

\- SBS supervision

\- certifications

\- partnerships

\- customer counts

\- testimonials

\- legal obligations

\- contact information



If data is not validated, use neutral wording such as:

\- Referencial

\- Información referencial

\- Pendiente de validación

\- Parámetro configurable

\- Condiciones sujetas a evaluación



Never imply:

\- creating an account = approval

\- simulation = approval

\- simulation = binding offer

\- approval is immediate

\- disbursement is guaranteed



Sprint 2 simulator results are referential.



The simulator may show:

\- capital

\- interest

\- applicable concepts

\- total referential



but must clearly communicate that final conditions depend on later validated parameters and evaluation.



Security language must remain practical and generic.



Allowed examples:

\- Usa canales oficiales.

\- No compartas tus códigos de acceso.

\- Revisa tu información antes de continuar.



Do not make unsupported claims such as:

\- seguridad bancaria

\- 100% seguro

\- fraude cero

\- supervisado por SBS



When unsure, prefer less specific copy rather than inventing product policy.
