Refine the existing Home screens inside:

`04 — Sprint 01 · Public & Auth`

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT redesign the approved Hero.
Do NOT modify Foundations or Components.
Do NOT remove the existing approved Home structure.
This task is a refinement pass to add more commercial depth, regional identity and branded visual richness.

Update ONLY:
- `Home / Desktop`
- `Home / Mobile`

OBJECTIVE

The current Home is structurally correct and aligned with the design system, but it still needs more commercial life and stronger brand expression below the Hero.

Improve the Home by adding a controlled amount of extra content and branded visual atmosphere.

The Home should feel:
- more complete
- more commercial
- more alive
- more regional
- more memorable

without becoming overloaded.

ADD / REFINE THESE AREAS

1. REGIONAL / INSTITUTIONAL SECTION
Add one section that reinforces regional identity and product positioning.

Suggested direction:
Heading:
`De San Martín, para San Martín`

Supporting idea:
A short institutional paragraph explaining that the experience is designed with clarity, closeness and a regional perspective.

This section should feel more editorial and institutional than functional.

Use:
- `SectionHeading`
- a supporting text block
- optional supporting image or visual composition if already available
- `Horizonte` as the preferred brand graphic resource here

IMPORTANT:
Use Horizonte intentionally as an atmospheric identity layer.
Do not let it overpower readability.

2. ALLIES SECTION
Add a section:
`Nuestros Aliados`

Purpose:
add trust and commercial credibility.

Structure:
- section heading
- short support text
- a clean row/grid of partner placeholders or neutral logo slots

If real partner logos do not exist, use clearly neutral placeholder blocks or generic partner-name placeholders, but keep them elegant and understated.

Do NOT invent legal claims or business relationships.

This section should feel orderly, calm and trust-building.

Prefer a clean light surface.
Topography Light may be used very subtly if helpful, but it is optional.

3. STRONGER FINAL CTA
Refine or replace the current final CTA area before the Footer.

Preferred direction:
Heading:
`Solicita tu préstamo aquí`
or a more polished equivalent such as:
`Comienza con una simulación`
or
`Da el siguiente paso`

Support text:
short, commercial, clear, non-aggressive.

Primary CTA:
`Solicitar préstamo`
or
`Simular ahora`

This section should feel more visually intentional than a simple plain block.

Use:
- Button instance
- controlled brand composition
- `La Ruta` as directional support
- optional low-density `Horizonte` if needed, but do not overload

The CTA section should feel like the last strong conversion moment before the Footer.

4. BETTER USE OF BRAND GRAPHICS
The current Home should use more of the approved graphic system below the Hero.

Apply with restraint:

- `Horizonte`
  → best for institutional/regional section

- `La Ruta`
  → best for guiding emphasis in CTA or transitions

- `Topografía`
  → only as subtle low-density background support

Do NOT combine all 3 strongly in one single section.

5. GENERAL POLISH
While making these additions, also refine:
- section rhythm
- spacing
- transitions between sections
- visual density
- composition balance
- commercial feel

The page should remain clean and premium, not busy.

The final Home should keep its current solid structure but feel more complete, more branded and more commercially alive through:
